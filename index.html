About Revoria

Revoria is an oldschool Tibia network built around separate worlds with clear purpose: fast PvP testing, long-term RPG progression and community-driven development.

The goal is simple: fair gameplay, stable worlds, controlled progression and updates based on real player testing instead of random changes.
