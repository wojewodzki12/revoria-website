About Revoria

Revoria is an oldschool Tibia network built around separate worlds with clear purpose: fast PvP testing, medium-rate progression and long-term RPG gameplay.

The goal is simple: fair gameplay, stable worlds, controlled progression and updates based on real player testing instead of random changes.

Fair Gameplay

No pay-to-win direction. Progress should feel earned.

Stable Worlds

Built around economy, retention and long-term balance.

Community Testing

Feedback and live testing help improve every world.
