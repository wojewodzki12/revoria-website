About Revoria

Revoria is an oldschool Tibia network built around separate worlds with clear purpose: fast PvP action, long-term RPG progression and community-driven development.

The goal is simple: fair gameplay, stable worlds, controlled progression and updates based on real player testing instead of random changes.

Fair Gameplay

No pay-to-win direction. Progress should feel earned.

Long-Term Worlds

Built around stability, economy and retention.

Community Testing

Feedback and live testing help improve every world.
